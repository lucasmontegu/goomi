# Goomi

Goomi turns the moment someone reaches for a blocked app into a short learning challenge. Content comes from a shared bank of open-data challenges and, for Plus subscribers, from their own study notes.

## Content

**Challenge**:
One short question the device can show offline during an interruption.
_Avoid_: Question (except for AI study), item, card

**Bank**:
The shared, server-built set of open-data challenges that devices sync and cache per language.
_Avoid_: Feed, catalog, content pool

**Material**:
A set of study notes the user added (PDF, photos, slides or pasted text) and the challenges made from it.
_Avoid_: Document, upload, file

**Study stage**:
One step of preparing a Material with AI on the server, in a fixed order.
_Avoid_: Step (that's a Study phase)

**Study phase**:
One of the four steps the user sees while a Material is prepared; each covers one or more Study stages.
_Avoid_: Stage

**Concept**:
The idea a challenge teaches; spaced repetition tracks memory per Concept, not per challenge.
_Avoid_: Topic, term

## Access

**Plus**:
The paid subscription (store entitlement `goomi_pro`) that unlocks AI study and higher Bank allowances.
_Avoid_: Pro, premium

**Install id**:
A random, per-install identity that lets a signed-out device sync the Bank against its own allowance.
_Avoid_: Device id, anonymous user

**Allowance**:
What a user or install may still consume in a period: Bank items per day, Materials, pages and closer reads per month, and AI spend.
_Avoid_: Quota (in UI copy), limit

**Closer read**:
Reading a page image with AI because the device couldn't find its text (handwriting, scans).
_Avoid_: OCR (in UI copy)
